Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

Lawn Mower Tycoon ("the game") is made by Lawn Mower Tycoon. This policy explains what information the game collects and how it is used.

What the game keeps on your device

Your progress, your settings and your company name are saved only on your device. They are not sent to us, and we can't see them. Deleting the game deletes them.

The game has no accounts, and it does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

Ads

The game offers optional ads that you choose to watch for an in-game reward. They are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • Device identifiers, including Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA), but only if you allow tracking when the game asks
  • Your IP address, and an approximate location worked out from it
  • Information about your device, such as its model, operating system and language
  • How you interact with the ads you see

If you choose not to allow tracking, you'll still see ads, but they will be less tailored to you. You can change this at any time in your iPhone's Settings, under Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Where the law requires it, Google asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown.

Google's use of this information is covered by Google's own privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy. You can learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Crash reports

If the game crashes, it may send a crash report to Sentry, our crash reporting service, so we can fix the problem. A report describes the error, your device model and operating system, and the game's version. It does not include your name, contacts or anything you've typed.

Notifications

If you allow them, the game schedules reminders on your iPhone, for example when your crew finishes a shift. They are created on your device, and no information is sent to us to do this.

Purchases

You can buy in-game cash with real money. Apple processes every payment. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the game only that a purchase was made, so the cash can be added.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we'll help remove it.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your information. Apart from the ad, crash reporting and payment providers described above, the game shares no information with anyone else.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
