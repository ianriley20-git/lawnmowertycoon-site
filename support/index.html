Help & Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email support@lawnmowertycoon.com. Please include your iPhone model and what happened, and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

Common questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your iPhone. There's no account, so deleting the game deletes your progress.

I bought a cash pack but didn't get the cash.

Close the game completely and open it again. A purchase that didn't finish is paid out the next time the game opens. If it still hasn't arrived, email us with the date of the purchase.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles every payment, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Do I have to watch ads?

No. Ads only play when you choose to watch one for a reward.

How do I turn off the music, sounds or notifications?

Open Settings from the gear on the Home screen. Music and sound effects each have their own volume, and notifications can be switched off.
